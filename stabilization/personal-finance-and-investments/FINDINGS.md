## Identity and typed-task seam

Finance is the source of truth for its domain records. The local context contract (`finance-context-read-v1`) is a read projection with versioned schema, purpose and destination metadata; its Shopping budget capability includes only one selected budget and its amount, currency, and period. Consent and source revision are rechecked. The read path is behind `FINANCE_CONTEXT_READ_ENABLED=false` by default. Existing Finance evidence covers synthetic Google-token verification and principal binding only; deployed identity, actual owner mapping, grant/revocation experience, and private service invocation remain open.

The older `PersonalAIClient.extract` candidate is separately disabled and has no HTTP adapter. Finance has no typed-task submission, destination execution, task persistence/idempotency record, or structured result-consumption path. The source-side budget record is therefore not an end-to-end delegated action. Suite-level shared identity and typed-task contracts remain coordinator decisions.
