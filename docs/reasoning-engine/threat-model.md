# Reasoning threat model

Assets include owner identity, private context and memory, current source
rights, provider budgets, original-intent receipts and experiment integrity.
Clients, tool content and candidate answers can be adversarial. Only verified
server context and current owning policy grant authority. The benchmark host
and scorer are trusted, while prepared task data and answers cross separate
closed boundaries. Content-free reports cross a further retention boundary.

| Abuse sequence | Owning rule | Local negative | Open proof and future owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Request max, pin research and ask for cross-app data or writes | Effort allocates work inside existing authority. Catalog effect classes are read-only or proposal-only. | Request/effort matrix, wrong-app denial and foreign slot excluded from preparation | Verified actor/app/workspace admission and exact current tool grant in RE-1/2, PI-5 and HXE-1 |
| Advertise zero price then charge or switch accounts | PI-3 requires exact account/operation eligibility and reservation. | Fake quota deny reaches zero fake attempts, cost claim remains unknown | Real billing/identity, alternate provider and reservation settlement in PI-3 and RE-1/2 |
| Source tells model to install a tool, override policy or read another app | Closed catalog, typed data, current source scope | Injected instruction passes only as inert content, foreign slot is withheld, fabricated citation vetoes | Native source trust, adapter isolation and malicious installed consumer in HXE-1/PI-5 and RE-2/3 |
| Revoke or revise source after preparation, replay or export | Revalidate current revision and disclosure right at the boundary | Simulated revoke-at-send and stale revision deny before attempt | Issuer-bound atomic revoke/send, history/export rights and restore in PI-5/MEM and RE-2 |
| Reuse key with new body, lose dispatch ack, cancel or cross deadline | HXE-3 and P19 keep original intent and uncertain receipts | Changed-body conflict, duplicate receipt observation, cancel/deadline and single uncertain attempt | Native crash, fencing, cancellation after dispatch, recovery and mixed-age restore in HXE-3/P19 and RE-2 |
| Leak gold, handpick holdouts or submit self-scores/citations | Seal fixed cohort, separate labels and closed oracle, check citation-to-slot/value | Gold and split absent from driver, malformed observations rejected, sealed mutation rejected, false citation blocked | Blind independently curated cohort and real-model generalization in RE-4 |
| Persist secrets or hidden reasoning via results, stdout, file or network | Retention projection and process/effect audit | Canary excluded from stored report, audit counts denied writes, subprocess and sockets | Independently verified native process/filesystem/secret fencing and deployment privacy in RE-5 |
| Turn simple task into unnecessary calls | One generation direct and costed check under matched cap | Direct effect count, excluded unsupported pairs and check counters | Real cost/benefit, sufficiency and calibrated promotion in RE-3/4 |
| Credit PAS for existing editor, shell or retries | External harness owns edits and lifecycle | Separate matched protocol with no local integration claim | Authorized independent paired study in RE-4/5 |

The synthetic negatives exercise the offline contracts. Deployed rights,
data erasure, strict-free settlement and native protections are separate
[release gates](../platform-independence/pi-cross-phase-gate-registry.md).
