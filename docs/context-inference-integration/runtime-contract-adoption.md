The candidate uses PAS revision `f5d12d82e19c864dcac6bd7fbaebc2c43a6c7d0c`, including the preview publication repair at `28bb98e4`. [R0 handoff](runtime-r0/R0-exit-review-and-next-phase-handoff.md)

## Decision

Adopt the published private `application-plan/v1` HTTP profile by reference. Python callers explicitly select it with `ApplicationPlan(Client(...))` from `personal-ai-sdk==0.2.0`. The external consumer contract remains **v1.2.0** and owns canonical create, status, detail and SSE semantics. The isolated host installs exactly two private POST routes at construction. The ordinary API and standalone bridge omit both routes. PAS owns preview assembly, confirmation binding, the routing-root claim and guarded dispatch. Reusing these authorities gives future TypeScript and native callers one wire contract and one continuation lifecycle. A second adapter specification or universal transport would duplicate consent and recovery rules and couple the caller to physical delivery. ## Authority and recovery

- PAS verifies the direct bearer ID token's issuer, subject, exact audience and effective authorized party, then resolves the operator-registered client/application and current membership for applicable workspace scope before protected reads. The legacy fixture marker selects nothing. The domain authorizes current records and revisions. PAS independently checks source grants and memory permissions for PAS access, the local runtime and the onward model recipient, including derived history. Travel retains writes and must check trip membership and the scoped `travel-context-read-v2` projection with `workspace_id=null`. Health retains writes and must check Firebase ownership, `health-context-read-v1` and granular `ai_use_allowed` and `cross_domain_use_allowed` consent. Federation and MCP grants each cover their own disclosure. [Identity middleware](../../backend/src/personal_ai/auth/middleware.py) · [Source and recipient authority](user-owned-application-inference.md#authorization-sequence) · [Domain handoff](runtime-r0/R0-exit-review-and-next-phase-handoff.md)
- The frozen offer binds the exact intent, branch, revisions, grant epochs, prepared payload, recipients and runtime/account registration. At release, PAS rechecks identity, client, source and memory authority and the claim. The paired runtime rechecks the exact binding, account, catalog and included-only evidence and requires current PAS authority under its send-start guard. PAS carries current authority through deltas, terminal output, stored detail and replay. Execution, catalog and receipt authority here are injected. A signature supplies integrity and authenticity. Confidential delivery requires its own protection. Revocation can still race the check-to-wire interval, and delivered bytes cannot be recalled. [Service](../../backend/src/personal_ai/services/external_turns/application_plan.py) · [Runtime fixture](../../backend/tests/app_context_support/application_plan.py)
- In private bounded-retention storage, journal the target URL, application, expected verified human and client, workspace, original keys, title, conversation ID, preview intent and exact confirmed body as available **before** their respective POSTs. Keep credentials separately, leave tokens on their bound devices and reauthenticate the binding before recovery. After an ambiguous continuation, including a disconnect, 429 or later 404 status, reconcile with original-key GETs and preserve the fence. Synchronous SDK success requires a matching SSE terminal, clean EOF and agreeing original-key status. Later GET recovery separately requires authorized detail. Deltas, request IDs, HTTP 200 and caller-supplied text supply no completion or billing proof. [Recovery example](../platform-independence/private-application-inference-v1.md#install-and-recover) · [Stream reader](../../sdk/python/src/personal_ai_sdk/client.py)
- Preserve the existing automatic 0.1.0 path and dispatch v1 canonical bytes, and preserve legacy-reader rejection of v2. Deny private downgrade, model substitution and paid fallback. On rollback, disable the private composition and retain unknown receipts, routing roots, exact claim witnesses, revocation epochs and caller journals. Retained fences aim at at most one app-originated physical attempt. Remote exactly-once delivery and provider-backed lookup remain unverified. ## Eligibility and promotion

Domain-only Travel and Health are the selected product defaults. The installed macOS 14+ OSS Electron example is conditional for a source-free proof. Non-Electron desktop and pure browser remain unverified. A solely user-controlled VM is conditional on transfer and persistence rules and unsuitable as the consumer default. The companion was rejected for browser-only Travel. Travel partner web and Health partner mobile are `blocked_external` until written terms name the app, approved host and custodian. Embedded Health is separately unverified on iOS and Android. Website identity, OS custody and a working sample establish only their specific scopes. Pooled tokens and operator-accessible per-user vaults are rejected. Developer API is disabled and out of scope pending separate consent, funding and an enforceable hard budget.
